Overview

The UVA Trading App is a full-stack peer-to-peer trading platform designed to help students exchange goods and reduce waste, promoting sustainable campus reuse. Built with Django and PostgreSQL, the application provides a complete ecosystem for cashless exchanges where users can easily list items, submit and track trade offers, and communicate securely via built-in direct messaging.

My Role & Contributions

Core Trading & Offer Workflow

  • Offer Lifecycle Management: Engineered the end-to-end trading logic, allowing users to submit offers on individual listings and track their status (pending, accepted, or denied) through a custom "My Trades" dashboard.
  • Direct Messaging System: Built and connected a database-backed direct messaging feature to facilitate secure, real-time communication between buyers and sellers.
  • Dynamic Trade UI: Developed standalone trade pages and dynamic "Trade Cards" that update their titles and UI states based on the current relationship and status of the offer.

User Identity & Profile Customization

  • Dynamic Profiles: Implemented comprehensive profile editing, allowing users to update their bio, university role, and trading interests.
  • Database Pre-population: Restructured the database models for "sustainability" and "trading interests" to pull from pre-populated options rather than requiring manual admin input, improving data consistency.
  • Role-Specific Experiences: Built custom routing logic to serve different landing page views based on the user's specific role at UVA.

Platform Moderation & Security

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Created a dedicated Moderator Dashboard that allows platform admins to view the entire user base, search for specific profiles by username or role, and actively moderate the platform.
  • Content Moderation: Gave moderators the ability to safely delete inappropriate posts and remove users, implementing confirmation modals to prevent accidental data loss.

UI/UX & Data Integrity

  • Real-Time Notifications: Designed and implemented a dynamic navigation bar badge that automatically updates with the user's count of pending trades.
  • Form Validation & Error Handling: Enforced strict data integrity by adding max-character constraints and required-field styling to forms, alongside custom error messages that display when invalid profile edits are attempted.
  • Post Management: Added intuitive front-end controls, including edit and delete buttons directly on the home page, allowing users to seamlessly manage their active listings.
