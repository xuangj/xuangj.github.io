WestWind Wildlife Site Build

WordPress, Elementor, PHP, HTML/CSS, Canva

Overview

Westwind Wildlife is a brick-and-mortar hunting supply store and the sister company to TagOut Technique. To help the business expand its physical retail footprint into the digital space, I developed a specialized WordPress platform that respects the Amish owners' religious restrictions regarding technology. The site functions as a comprehensive digital catalog where hunters can browse premium supplies and seamlessly submit orders. By employing a customized tech-free fulfillment pipeline, the online platform drives retail growth without requiring the owners to continuously manage a standard digital storefront.

Responsive Design & Catalogs

Built fully responsive catalog views across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints to provide seamless accessibility for hunters browsing equipment in the field.

westwindwildlife.com

My Role & Contributions

  • Client Consultation & UX/UI Design: Conducted stakeholder interviews to gather requirements for translating their physical retail experience into a digital format. Designed structural wireframes using Canva, mapping out an accessible and intuitive user journey that was presented and approved prior to development.
  • Custom E-Commerce Pipeline:Engineered a customized WooCommerce workflow tailored to the client's offline operational needs. Designed a system where customer shopping carts are converted into downloadable PDF quotes and automatically dispatched via email to the business for traditional processing.
  • Frontend WordPress Development: Built the fully responsive website utilizing WordPress and the Elementor page builder, ensuring hunting enthusiasts could easily navigate product catalogs, view store details, and submit orders across all device types.
  • Marketing Integration: Set up and integrated Mailchimp to capture leads and manage the store's newsletter, establishing a communication channel to drive ongoing engagement with their local and digital customer base.
