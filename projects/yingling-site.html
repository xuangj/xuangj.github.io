Yingling Builders Site Transition

WordPress, Elementor, PHP, HTML/CSS, Canva

Overview

Yingling Builders is a custom home building company delivering high-quality craftsmanship and a personalized home-building experience. Their original WordPress site ran on a rigid, outdated theme, making it incredibly difficult for their team to maintain, update, or expand. To solve this, I completely rebuilt the site from the ground up using Elementor page builder. The result is a modernized, highly responsive, and visually engaging digital portfolio that allows the client to easily showcase their custom homes, manage their client portal, and update services without relying on constant developer intervention.

Responsive Design

Built fully responsive views across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints to provide seamless accessibility for hunters browsing equipment in the field.

My Role & Contributions

  • Full-Scale Website Rebuild: Designed and developed a complete overhaul of the legacy website using WordPress and the Elementor plugin, transforming a rigid, hard-to-maintain theme into a flexible and scalable digital platform.
  • Content Migration & Structure: Successfully migrated existing copy, service descriptions, and portfolio imagery, reorganizing the site architecture to prominently feature their work, service areas, and partnership with Wausau Homes.
