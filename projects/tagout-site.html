Overview

TagOut Technique is a specialized e-commerce and land management platform built for an Amish-owned hunting supply business. The company provides premium food plot seeds, herbicides, hunting blinds, and property consulting to help hunters transform their land and attract better prey. The core challenge of this project was engineering a digital storefront that respected the client's religious beliefs and anti-big-box philosophy, which restrict direct, continuous interaction with modern technology. To bridge this gap, I designed a custom e-commerce pipeline that allows customers to shop online seamlessly, but converts their cart into a PDF quote that is automatically routed via email to the client's offline order-processing system.

My Role & Contributions

  • Client Consultation & UX/UI Design: Led requirement-gathering interviews to deeply understand the client's unique business needs, target audience, and technological constraints. Translated these constraints into a comprehensive user interface wireframe using Canva, which was pitched to and approved by the client.
  • Custom E-Commerce Pipeline: Researched and architected an innovative order-processing workflow alongside my team to accommodate the tech-free requirements. Integrated WooCommerce for the customer-facing storefront and implemented a custom "quote" feature that dynamically generates a PDF cart summary and emails it directly to the client at the push of a button.
  • Frontend WordPress Development: Built the complete responsive website utilizing WordPress and the Elementor page builder, ensuring an intuitive and accessible browsing experience for hunting enthusiasts looking for gear and land management services.
  • Marketing Integration: Configured and connected Mailchimp to handle the company's newsletter subscriptions, establishing a scalable foundation for their ongoing community building and customer outreach efforts.
